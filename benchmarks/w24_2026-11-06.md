and a system scope with AllowedCPUs=2, MemoryMax=10G and MemorySwapMax=0.

30 rounds/process, 1% practical margin, workload `w24-fixed256-v1`.

## Rectangle validation

Frozen validation sources: canonical imazen-26 commit
`187fbf338ce08e8e6654db7f04ddae58d5263da2`, K1000-represented whole images with
validation-only supplements: 50 photo, 50 screen, 50 line/document and 107 mixed.
No training, fitting or ground-truth quality claims. The 257 originals were
fetched from preserved manifest URLs, hash-checked before evaluation. Derived
source sizes use longest sides 64, 256, 1024 and 4096 only when smaller than the
original, plus native size: 992 cases. Fixed-shape inference resizes to 256×256.

Both fixed and fast paths match tract's salient bounds at relative thresholds
0.3/0.5 and the shared zenlayout 0.2.2 solver's crops for nine aspect ratios in
Minimal and Maximal modes. Zero source- or map-pixel edge movement in all 1,984
candidate comparisons; zero crop-presence mismatches. The user-approved limit
is three pixels per edge on the 128×128 map, x normalized by source width and y
by source height. Largest map-value difference: fixed 9.596347809e-6;
fast sigmoid 1.043081284e-5. These are runtime differences, not ground-truth MAE.

Reproduce with `just crop-rectangles CORPUS INPUTS.tsv NEW_REPORT.tsv 3`.

## Limits

This is one CPU class, one model and one fixed inference size. No other-host
speed claim, inference-only memory claim or executable-size comparison is made.
Depth fusion has no demonstrated crop-quality benefit yet. Approximation gates
apply to saliency geometry, not image/depth decoder pixels. Quantization and
retraining are not implemented. ARM/Windows/i686 consumer CI remains pending.
