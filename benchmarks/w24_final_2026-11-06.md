## Five-process fixed confirmation

`W24_CPU_SET=2 just micro-confirm`, under run-heavy and a system scope with
AllowedCPUs=2, MemoryMax=10G, MemorySwapMax=0. Five fresh processes, 30 fixed
rounds each, 1% margin, workload `w24-fixed256-v1`; uniform placement verified.
Frequency was not verified. Input packing and output materialization included.

| Runtime | Warm descriptive mean | Model construction mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| tract 0.23.10 | 4.73 ms | 19.18 ms |
| fixed custom | 4.12 ms | 1.37 ms |
| custom fast sigmoid | 3.88 ms | Same constructor, not separately measured |

Family-adjusted paired process intervals:
- Fixed versus tract: -16.1303% to -9.8199%, improvement.
- Fast versus tract: -25.8855% to -9.6274%, improvement.
- Fast versus fixed: -16.5855% to +6.1653%, inconclusive.
- Custom versus tract construction: -93.6675% to -91.9460%, improvement.

Construction is repeated model loading/packing in the benchmark process, not
a fresh-process startup-latency measurement. The whole suite exits 2 because
fast versus fixed is inconclusive. No baseline was promoted. Full text export:
[confirmation log](w24_final_confirm_2026-11-06.log); immutable raw logs and
packets are external. `run-heavy: done rc=2 54s | peak-RSS 0.67GiB |
min-avail 26975MiB | peak-load 0.92`. This RSS includes compilation/supervision.

## Correctness and geometry

The final source again produced zero salient-region and final-crop edge
movement in all 1,984 candidate comparisons: 257 validation originals,
992 source-size cases, fixed and fast modes. The approved gate is three
128x128 map pixels per edge, normalized separately by source width/height.
The source manifests and sampling protocol are in
[the initial record](w24_2026-11-06.md) and [data provenance](../DATA_PROVENANCE.md).
The final report remains external. No mask or human crop ground truth was fit.

The complete operator, scalar/SIMD, strided-input and fusion checks passed on
actual AVX-512 hardware with `ZENSALLY_REQUIRED_TIER=v4` required. The runtime
also passes std/no_std tests and bare-metal aarch64-unknown-none compilation.
Native depth matches the independent oracle's 442,368 codes. No decoder pixel
expectation, quality threshold or pre-existing ignore was relaxed.
