# Synthetic photo art direction

Make believable photographs for the product and situation. Variation should
emerge naturally across customers, activities, group sizes, clothing and settings.
Do not turn casting into an identity checklist or insert medical traits or
mobility aids merely to signal variety.

Review the concept before generation. Check the associations between the person,
product and packaging copy. The rejected skincare source explicitly requested a
medical trait and then received Brightening copy; that pairing was poor art
direction. Use restrained, appropriate product copy.

Review new outputs at full size before positive training/reference admission.
Check natural hands and limb connections, how products are held or attached,
and whether objects have separate physical surfaces, straps and contact shadows.
The rejected watch image appeared fused to the wrist. A replacement must show
a clearly external case and a band visibly encircling the wrist.

The owner permits minor generated quirks for this crop network. That does not
remove the need for social judgment, coherent product geometry and a scene
that reads naturally. Preserve rejected originals and record why; do not quietly
overwrite or count them as approved examples. Replacements inherit source
families and roles. These reviews are art direction, not crop-quality gold or
complete face-coverage certification.
